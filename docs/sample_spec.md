## 1. Problem statement

- **RAG pipelines degrade on Vietnamese legal text with cross-references** — Standard RAG treats each chunk independently and fails to follow references between legal provisions, causing incomplete context and incorrect answers.
- **Cross-references between Vietnamese legal texts are an explicit challenge** — The user explicitly highlights provisions with cross-references between documents as the special focus for RAG improvement.

## 2. Research questions

- **How can cross-references be automatically extracted from Vietnamese legal texts?** — What parsing and linking methods can identify references to other articles, clauses, or laws in Vietnamese legal language?
- **What retrieval architecture best incorporates cross-reference structure into RAG?** — Should retrieval use graph traversal, query expansion, or reranking to include referenced provisions alongside directly matched chunks?
- **How should end-to-end RAG be evaluated on cross-reference-heavy legal queries?** — What metrics and datasets can capture whether the system retrieves all necessary referenced articles and generates legally faithful answers?

## 4. Research gap

- **Current Vietnamese legal retrieval methods ignore explicit cross-references between provisions**
  - _What prior work achieved_: Recent Vietnamese legal retrieval systems improve data processing, normalize Reciprocal Rank Fusion, and apply active reranking to boost retrieval performance on legal QA benchmarks (source: 8991a8c9).
  - _Remaining limitation_: These systems treat legal documents as independent chunks and do not follow explicit cross-references such as 'as stipulated in Article X of Law Y', so retrieved context may omit mandatory provisions outside the directly matched chunk.
  - _Why that limitation matters_: Legal answers often depend on multiple linked provisions; missing a referenced article leads to incomplete context and incorrect legal interpretations, undermining trust in automated legal assistance.
  - _Experiment that would test it_: Create a set of Vietnamese legal queries whose answers require following one or more cross-references, and compare the proportion of necessary provisions retrieved by the current system against oracle expansion using cross-reference links.

- **Cross-reference resolution in Vietnamese legal knowledge graphs does not handle temporal validity and ambiguous references**
  - _What prior work achieved_: An ontology-driven knowledge graph for Vietnamese legal QA (source: b79e46ba) encodes cross-reference relations and uses graph-aware retrieval to provide article-level citations.
  - _Remaining limitation_: That knowledge graph is restricted to three law domains and does not resolve references that are ambiguous (e.g., pointing to multiple possible articles) or that may become outdated when laws are amended or replaced.
  - _Why that limitation matters_: Vietnamese laws are frequently revised; a system that cannot track whether a referenced provision is still in force risks citing repealed or superseded text, leading to legally invalid answers.
  - _Experiment that would test it_: Construct a test set of cross-references with known temporal status (active, repealed, ambiguous) and measure the proportion of correctly resolved references by the existing graph vs a version-aware resolver.

## 5. Proposed approach

We propose a cross-reference-aware RAG pipeline for Vietnamese legal documents. First, a cross-reference graph builder parses statutes and extracts explicit references to articles, clauses, and laws, resolving them to nodes in a versioned knowledge graph. Second, a version-aware resolver assigns temporal status (active/repealed/ambiguous) to each reference. Third, a reference-aware retrieval module expands the query with linked provisions and reranks chunks by combining semantic similarity with graph proximity. Fourth, we construct a benchmark of cross-reference-heavy legal questions with annotations of the minimum set of required provisions, and evaluate retrieval recall and answer faithfulness against standard dense retrieval.

## 6. Expected contributions

- **Cross-reference graph builder for Vietnamese legal texts** — We define a parsing and linking component that extracts explicit statutory references and constructs a graph of nodes for laws, chapters, sections, articles, clauses, and points, with edges representing cross-reference relations.
- **Version-aware reference resolver** — We implement a resolver that assigns temporal status to cross-references by consulting consolidated and historical law texts, distinguishing active, repealed, and ambiguous references.
- **Reference-aware retrieval module** — We develop a retriever that uses the cross-reference graph to expand queries and rerank chunks based on graph proximity and semantic similarity, ensuring referenced provisions are included before generation.
- **Benchmark with cross-reference annotations** — We construct a dataset of Vietnamese legal questions annotated with the minimum set of provisions required to answer, including those reached through cross-references, enabling systematic evaluation of reference-aware retrieval.
- **Evaluation protocol for reference-aware retrieval** — We define metrics for retrieval recall and citation precision on cross-reference-heavy queries, along with an ablation plan to isolate the effect of reference expansion.

## 8. Experimental protocol

- **TN1 — Reference extraction precision and recall benchmark**
  - Compare the proposed cross-reference graph builder against a rule-based regex extractor
  - On a manually annotated sample of 500 Vietnamese legal references held out from development
  - Metric: precision and recall of extracted statutory references, reported as mean over three annotation adjudication rounds
  - Success: precision >= 0.90 and recall >= 0.85 on the held-out sample
  - _Tests claim_: The cross-reference graph builder extracts explicit statutory references with precision at least 0.90 and recall at least 0.85 on a manually annotated sample of 500 Vietnamese lega

- **TN2 — Temporal status classification accuracy**
  - Compare the version-aware resolver with a non-versioned resolver that uses only current law text
  - On a held-out set of 200 references annotated by legal experts with temporal statuses
  - Metric: classification accuracy for active, repealed, and ambiguous statuses
  - Success: accuracy >= 0.80 and exceeds the non-versioned baseline by at least 5 points
  - _Tests claim_: The version-aware resolver assigns correct temporal status (active, repealed, ambiguous) to cross-references with accuracy at least 0.80 on a held-out set of 200 annotated referenc

- **TN3 — Reference-aware retrieval Recall@10**
  - Compare reference-aware retrieval (with cross-reference expansion and graph-reranking) against dense retrieval without expansion
  - On 300 cross-reference-heavy Vietnamese legal queries from the newly constructed benchmark
  - Metric: Recall@10, mean and standard deviation across three random seeds
  - Success: mean Recall@10 of reference-aware method exceeds the dense baseline by at least 10 points
  - _Tests claim_: Reference-aware retrieval using cross-reference expansion improves Recall@10 by at least 10 points over dense retrieval without expansion on cross-reference-heavy Vietnamese legal 

- **TN4 — Citation precision with reference-expanded context**
  - Compare a standard RAG pipeline (dense retrieval + fine-tuned Vietnamese legal LLM) against the same pipeline but with reference-expanded context
  - On 200 generated answers from the cross-reference benchmark, judged by legal experts
  - Metric: citation precision — proportion of cited provisions that truly support the answer
  - Success: citation precision improves by at least 15 points and the increase is significant under a paired bootstrap test at p<0.05; generation is run three times with different seeds to estimate variance
  - _Tests claim_: Providing reference-expanded context to the generator increases citation precision in generated legal answers by at least 15 points over baseline RAG without expansion.

## 9. Baselines and metrics

Baselines: BM25 (Anserini defaults) for retrieval; dense retrieval (multilingual sentence transformer fine-tuned on Vietnamese legal text, e.g., paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2); rule-based regex extractor for reference extraction; non-versioned resolver (current law only); standard RAG with fine-tuned PhoGPT-7B. Metrics: precision, recall, accuracy, Recall@10, nDCG@10, citation precision.

## 10. Ablation plan

Remove cross-reference expansion from the retrieval module while keeping graph-reranking: threatens claim 2 and claim 4 if recall or citation precision drops. Remove graph-proximity reranking but keep query expansion: threatens claim 2 if recall decreases. Replace the cross-reference graph builder with the regex extractor: threatens claim 1 if precision/recall falls below thresholds. Remove the version-aware historical consultation from the resolver: threatens claim 3 if accuracy drops below baseline difference.

## 12. Risks and limitations

The held-out reference sample is drawn from the same Vietnamese law corpus as development, so leakage of document-specific patterns may inflate extraction metrics; we partition by laws to mitigate. The dense retrieval baseline may be insufficiently tuned (e.g., single learning rate) and thus underperform, exaggerating the improvement of our method. Human expert annotations are limited and may have subjectivity; we report inter-annotator agreement. We use a separate development split for all tuning decisions; no model is trained on the held-out evaluation sets. All experiments use a single random seed for training except retrieval which uses three; generation uses three seeds as described, but other components may still lack multi-seed robustness.

## 13. Open issues

- **Is there a public benchmark with Vietnamese legal cross-reference annotations for evaluating reference-aware RAG?** — Is there an existing public benchmark that annotates cross-reference chains and the minimum set of linked provisions needed to answer a Vietnamese legal query? If no such benchmark exists, what would be required to build one?
- **How to handle temporal validity of cross-references** — Laws are amended and replaced; a cross-reference may point to a provision that is no longer in force. How should the system maintain version-aware links?
- **How to disambiguate ambiguous legal references** — Some references point to multiple articles or use vague language; resolution may require semantic understanding beyond string matching.
